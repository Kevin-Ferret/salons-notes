# Salon notes

A small offline web app for planning and taking notes at the Salon des Vins des Vignerons Indépendants (Paris Porte de Versailles, 10–13 December 2026).

- Open the site on your phone and add it to the home screen. After the first visit it works without a connection.
- Notes are stored only in the phone's browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Export your notes as `vignerons.md` from the Data tab after the salon, and load a previous file there to bring back past ratings.

The exhibitor list (654 winemakers, with stands) comes from the organiser's public list, pulled on 18 November 2026.
